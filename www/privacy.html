SweatCircle Privacy Policy & Terms

Effective Date: October 16, 2026 · App Store & Google Play Compliant

1. Overview & Zero-Sale Commitment

SweatCircle is designed as an accountability and cooperative streak fitness tracker for small private groups of friends and family. We respect your privacy: we do not sell your personal, health, or workout data to any third party or advertiser.

2. Health Data Handling (Apple HealthKit & Google Health Connect)

SweatCircle accesses health metrics through Apple HealthKit (on iOS) and Google Health Connect (on Android) strictly with your explicit permission.

  • Metrics Read: Active calories (kcal), exercise/workout duration (minutes), daily step count, and average heart rate (BPM).
  • Purpose: Used solely to verify workout completion, award group streak points, and stamp live telemetry onto your photo proof card.
  • Disclosures: Health data is never transmitted to advertising networks or data brokers.

3. Camera & Photos

SweatCircle accesses your device camera or photo library solely to let you upload same-day proof of a workout or meal. Photos are visible only to fellow members of the specific circle you belong to.

4. User Content Moderation & Safety

Users have access to in-app reporting ("Report Post") to flag objectionable or inappropriate images, which immediately conceals the content.

5. Account & Data Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))

You have the right to delete your account, friend circles, workout logs, and health records at any time directly inside the app under Settings > Delete My Account & All Stored Data.

6. Contact

For questions regarding privacy or data handling, contact: support@sweatcircle.app.
